My replication

This repository recreates the core idea with four expert modules corresponding to Language, Logic, Social, and World reasoning, plus a router that assigns token representations to an expert. The implementation includes expert training, a MiCRo layer, a T5-style transformer integration, and routing-visualization code.

Independent clean-room replication for research and educational purposes.
